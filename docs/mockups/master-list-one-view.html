How it replaces the two tabs
  • One list of items. Each row shows who sells it and the price, with ★ for the preferred shop.
  • Suppliers live on the left as a filter. Tap one to see its card (contact, shop link, Edit) and only its items. The old "request all low items" shortcut comes back here as one button.
  • Search covers both: typing "cozii" finds the supplier and its items.
  • "No supplier yet" shows items nobody has linked, so gaps are easy to fill.
  • Phone: the left list becomes an "All suppliers ▾" dropdown above the table.
  • Tapping an item still opens the item drawer; tapping a supplier chip on a row jumps to that supplier.

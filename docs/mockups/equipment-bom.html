Equipment by product (BOM)

Instead of numbered sets (SET1, SET2…), each product we rent out (LCA, What's in my Bag, Tally…) has a Bill of Materials: the list of what goes in the box. On a Won job you add the product, open it, and pick the actual units (CAM-007, PRT-002…) for each line. Sample data; the real product list comes from you.

1 · WHAT CHANGES

Now: sets

  • A unit is put in SET1 / SET2 / SET3 and the set goes to a job.
  • The set doesn't say what should be in it, so nobody can see what's missing.
  • Sets are fixed groups; swapping one camera means editing the set.

New: product + BOM

  • Each product has a BOM: “LCA = 1 camera, 1 printer, 1 laptop, 2 lights, 1 router + cable bag”.
  • On a job: ＋ Add product → LCA. It expands into those lines; pick a real unit for each.
  • You see at a glance: 5/7 packed, what's missing, what came back.
  • Any free unit of the right type can fill a line. No fixed sets to maintain.

HOW IT WORKS ON A JOB
  • ＋ Add product lists your products. The job's product type (“LCA + Film”) is suggested first.
  • Each product expands into its BOM lines; collapse it when done.
  • pick… shows free units of that type (on the rack, not on another job those dates).
  • Two LCA booths? Add LCA twice: LCA #1, LCA #2.
  • Send out moves every picked unit to “On event · #131” (replaces SET1/EV).
  • Return per line or all: back to its rack slot. Not returned → Lost / Repair.

5 · ON THE UNIT ITSELF (YOUR SCREENSHOT)

  • “Equipment set” (SET1/SET2/SET3/＋SET4) is removed.
  • “Where is it?” stays: Rack / Factory / On event, plus Repair / Lost / Retired.
  • On event shows “#131 · LCA #1 · Camera”: which job, which product, which line. Set from the job, not here.

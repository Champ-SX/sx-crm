Requests & POs: what each one is for

Every request names what it's for: General, Stock refill or Project. The name is required and the field guides people on what to write. Both lists then show what it's for, the branch, the items and the Needed by date. Sample data.

4 · RULES

  • For: General (default) · Stock refill · Project. Old job-linked requests stay as they are.
  • Name required for every type. The field's placeholder explains what to write, and Submit is blocked until it's filled.
  • Requests list: name as the title, first items, For chip, Deliver to (branch colour), total, Needed by (soon / late), PO, status.
  • POs list: requests on the PO (For + name + REQ), every delivery branch, the earliest Needed by.
  • Phone: the same information stacks inside each row (title → items → For · branch · Needed by).

"Not stocked" items: your choice
  • Option 1 · keep them on Stock (shown above): they appear with a dashed "not stocked" tag and a Request button instead of Reorder. One list for everything we buy. A "Not stocked" chip filters them.
  • Option 2 · hide them from Stock: Stock shows only counted items. Project-only items are found from the Request search and from each supplier's list. Cleaner Stock, but they have no home page of their own.
  • You can decide later: start with Option 1, and if the "not stocked" rows feel noisy, they can be hidden by default behind the chip.

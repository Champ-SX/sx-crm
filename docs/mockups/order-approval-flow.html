6 · RULES

  • Under ฿10,000 including VAT: approved straight away when submitted, and the requester sees "Approved · to order" immediately.
  • ฿10,000 or more: "Wait for approval". Champ gets an in-app notification and an email. Nobody can add it to a PO until it's approved.
  • Approve: the request becomes "Approved · to order" and the requester is notified. Reject requires a reason and notifies the requester, who can edit and resubmit.
  • Editing items after approval: if the new total is still under the limit, or no higher than the approved amount, the request stays approved. Otherwise it goes back to "Wait for approval".
  • Quick 1-item refills follow the same rule (most are under ฿10K, so they are approved automatically).
  • Receive is unchanged: per line or Receive all on the PO. Received quantities top up stock.
  • Requests Champ creates follow the same rule. At ฿10K or more Champ approves them with one click, so the approval is still recorded.

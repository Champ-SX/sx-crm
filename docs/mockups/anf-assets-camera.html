Tap a chip → opens the asset (Drive link, or the preset card) in a side panel without leaving the task.

How it works
  • Assets belong to a room (or "All branches" for shared ones), not to a task. Each gets a short ID: GA- for graphics, CAM- / FL- for each room's camera and flash preset.
  • Tasks reference assets: “＋ Link asset” in the drawer, or type # in any sub-task report to mention one. Chips show live status, tap to open.
  • Asset page shows where it's used: “Used in: Graduation campaign, Xmas roll-out”.
  • Sub-task reports take text + photos (same photo upload as activity notes). Photos show as thumbnails, tap to enlarge. Each report also lands in Activity.
  • Presets keep history: every edit is a new version with who and when.
  • The per-task Graphics tab goes away; graphics already added to tasks move into the library under their branch.
Decide
  • Calendar vs Timeline: they overlap (both show tasks by date). Proposal: remove Calendar, keep Timeline (better for multi-week work) and add a "This week" quick button.
  • One preset per room, or several per room (e.g. "Day" / "Night", or per camera body)?
  • Who can edit presets: anyone on the board, or Ops lead / admin only?
